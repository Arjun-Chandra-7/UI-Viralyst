Common questions

What does Viralyst analyze?

Viralyst performs multimodal analysis on short-form vertical video. It examines spoken dialogue syntax, visual framing transitions, kinetic caption pacing, audio rhythm cadence, and edit cut frequency to isolate repeatable creative structures.

How does Viralyst learn my brand?

Viralyst reviews your existing content library, target audience positioning, and creative parameters. It maps which format archetypes align with your brand voice before generating structural recommendations.

What kind of content does Viralyst study?

Viralyst focuses on short-form vertical video across Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts, analyzing recurring retention and narrative patterns across diverse creator categories.

What does Manager do?

Manager synthesizes identified patterns into clear creative directions. Instead of open-ended brainstorming, it suggests specific format archetypes, opening hook angles, and pacing blueprints tailored to your upcoming shoot.

Can Viralyst help me decide what to create next?

Yes. By comparing emerging format patterns with your recent content outputs, Viralyst highlights creative opportunities and suggests structured briefs—including hook concepts, visual resets, and pacing notes—ready for production.

How does Viralyst use performance feedback?

When you track how your published videos perform, Viralyst refines its brand model. Formats that drive viewer engagement are prioritized, while underperforming structures are systematically adjusted or deprioritized.

Is Viralyst a content generator or an intelligence system?

Viralyst is a content intelligence and creative direction system, not an automated generative tool. It provides structural blueprints, hook architectures, and directorial briefs so creators and production teams can shoot authentic, high-quality content.
