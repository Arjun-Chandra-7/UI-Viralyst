## 3. New Viralyst Object Language

### 1. Pure Reel Frame
- Pure 9:16 vertical media artifact with rounded borders and zero hardcover thickness.
- Features:
  - Full-bleed video frame with live playhead or camera HUD (`REC  00:04:12`).
  - Active hook banner: `THE CONTRARIAN OPEN`.
  - Frequency waveform bars in Media Cyan (`#20D5E5`) and Acid Lime (`#CBFF4A`).
  - Bottom timeline scrubber with current timestamp (`00:24`).

### 2. Signal
- Extracted short-form intelligence markers displayed as tactile chips:
  - `[SIGNAL: CONTRARIAN OPEN]`
  - `[CUT @ 0.8s]`
  - `[AUDIO SYNC: TRENDING]`
  - `[RETENTION: 96%]`
  - `FAST RESET @ 3.4s` (tilted floating sticker)

### 3. The Content Stack
- Three oversized vertical Reel frames layered in z-space:
  - Left: Raw video capture tilted -11° with viewfinder reticle.
  - Right: Audio spectrogram tilted +9° with 142 BPM speech sync bars.
  - Center: Upright focus Reel with active signal breakdown and play reticle.

### 4. The Content Blueprint
- The deliverable replacing the physical subscription box.
- Contains 4 operational modules:
  1. `01 / HOOK SCRIPT`: Contrarian premise & curiosity gap.
  2. `02 / FRAME PACING`: 0.8s micro-cuts with tactile visual proof before dialogue.
  3. `03 / AUDIO CADENCE`: 142 BPM speech sync & seamless loop return.
  4. `04 / CALL TO ACTION`: Comment triggers designed for debate and replays.
