# VIRALYST PRODUCT TRUTH — DEFINITIVE SYSTEM CONTRACT

## 1. Executive Summary
This document establishes the verified, indisputable product truth for Viralyst based directly on the parent repository implementation across `Client Brain`, `Extractor`, `Script Writer`, `Music SFX`, `RAG`, `Discriminator`, and `Vid-Editor`.

All landing page copy, visual artifacts, interactive demonstrations, and metadata must adhere strictly to these verified capabilities. Any claim, badge, metric, or feature not categorized under **REAL** or clearly designated as **ILLUSTRATIVE** is banned.

## 2. Feature Classification: Real vs Planned vs Illustrative vs Unsupported

### A. REAL (Verified Codebase Capabilities)
1. **Multimodal Short-Form Analysis**:
   - Ingestion of TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.
   - Frame-by-frame visual decomposition and scene splitting.
   - Transcript extraction with word-level timestamps.
   - Pacing & cut cadence calculation (e.g. cut frequency, silence intervals).
   - Audio spectrogram and speech delivery cadence (BPM / cadence alignment).
2. **Client Brain (Context & Voice Modeling)**:
   - Structured evidence-backed client profiles stored in SQLite/SQLAlchemy.
   - Audience segment definitions, competitor profiles, and brand guidelines.
   - Goal and constraint enforcement (e.g. tone boundaries, topic restrictions).
3. **Pattern Retrieval & RAG**:
   - Structural fingerprinting of high-performing video formats.
   - Vector similarity search over verified short-form frameworks.
   - Hook classification and format taxonomy mapping.
4. **Script Intelligence & Blueprint Compiler**:
   - Script generation scoped to verified client evidence and format fingerprints.
   - Multi-beat script blueprints: Hook, Tactile Proof, Context Pivot, Call to Action.
   - Explicit visual cues and audio cues mapped to each script line.
5. **Video Assembly & Kinetic Editing**:
   - Kinetic captions styling and automated transcript alignment.
   - Cut timing adjustments matching audio cadence.

### B. PLANNED (Architecturally Designed, Not Yet Shipped)
- Live multi-agent autonomous iteration loops.
- Client Brain direct live web-scraping provider.
- Native mobile companion application (NO app store live downloads currently exist).

### C. ILLUSTRATIVE (Allowed ONLY as Explicit Design Demonstrations)
- Example format archetype names (*Contrarian Hook, Fast Demo, Story → Payoff, Myth → Proof*).
- Conceptual Signal Core layer separations (*Visual Frame, Transcript Hook, Caption Layer, Audio Track, Pacing Timeline*).
- Demo question/answer interactions (*"What should we make next?"*).

### D. UNSUPPORTED (STRICTLY BANNED)
- **Fake Retention & Views Statistics**: e.g., "3x completion rate", "94% retention", "100M views".
- **Fake Creator Testimonials & Headshots**: Any fabricated persona, creator portrait, or customer quote.
- **Fake Annual Awards**: e.g., "Members' Choice", "Book of the Year", fabricated years (2024/2025/2026).
- **App Store & Google Play Badges**: Neither iOS nor Android apps are available on public app stores.
- **Book-Club & Subscription Terms**: "member", "book club", "box delivery", "monthly read".
- **Third-Party Publication Logos**: Unverified logos of news outlets (MSNBC, Publishers Weekly, etc.).
