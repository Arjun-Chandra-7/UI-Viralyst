## 1. The Three Signature Visual Primitives

### Primitive 01 — The Reel Specimen
* **Concept:** An art-directed vertical artifact representing actual short-form video (9:16 aspect ratio).
* **Strict Constraints:**
  * NOT a smartphone frame, laptop mockup, browser frame, or dashboard box.
  * ZERO fake platform UI (no simulated likes, share counts, comment bubbles, or platform chrome).
  * The media itself is the hero: high-contrast framing, subtle edge highlight, crisp editorial typography, dynamic internal timeline.
* **Six Art-Directed Content Archetypes:**
  1. `Specimen A — Contrarian Open`: Oversized typography-driven hook with bold contrasting statements.
  2. `Specimen B — Product Proof`: Macro close-up demonstration with immediate tactile proof.
  3. `Specimen C — Founder POV`: Cinematic editorial talking-head composition with tight vertical framing.
  4. `Specimen D — Screen First`: High-density interface/code workflow capture with illuminated cursor tracks.
  5. `Specimen E — Story Payoff`: Narrative B-roll pacing with synchronized dual-state subtitles.
  6. `Specimen F — Myth / Proof`: Two-state split composition resolving a common creator misconception.

### Primitive 02 — The V-Lens
* **Concept:** A 2.5D analytical scanning frame derived from the Viralyst "V" chevron emblem.
* **Mechanism:**
  * Moves across or vertically over a Reel Specimen.
  * Outside the Lens: The Reel appears as raw, organic short-form media.
  * Inside the Lens: The internal structural layers become illuminated:
    * `Hook Syntax`: Semantic text highlighting curiosity gap.
    * `Kinetic Caption Timing`: Micro-timestamps aligned to word delivery.
    * `Audio Spectrum`: 142 BPM cadence and frequency dynamics.
    * `Edit / Cut Traces`: Precise timestamp cut points (`0.0s`, `0.8s`, `1.6s`).
  * As the lens advances: Key structural signals persist as elegant trace lines.
* **Material Character:**
  * Crisp vector rails with laser-etched V-aperture.
  * Physical, precise, graphical, premium.
  * NOT sci-fi holographic, neon, or cyberpunk.

### Primitive 03 — The Signal Ribbon
* **Concept:** A multi-track editorial DNA strip that generates alongside the analyzed Reel.
* **Structure:**
  * Track 1: `WORDS` (Hook sentence & curiosity gap)
  * Track 2: `VISUAL` (Framing changes & macro focal resets)
  * Track 3: `CAPTIONS` (Word cadence & font velocity)
  * Track 4: `AUDIO` (Frequency intensity & beat sync)
  * Track 5: `EDIT` (Cut frequency & scene transitions)

## 2. Color Palette Foundation

```text
INK           #0B0B0F   Deep obsidian foundation for contrast & darkness
PAPER         #F4F0E8   Warm editorial tactile parchment
CORAL RED     #FF5146   Primary brand energy & hook identifier
SIGNAL BLUE   #315CFF   Audio, rhythm, & structural connections
HOT ROSE      #F53F83   Captions & kinetic timing indicators
PALE LILAC    #C5BCFF   Subtle structural surfaces & borders
WARM YELLOW   #F5D45A   Editorial high-impact highlights
ACID (Micro)  #BEFF47   Strictly reserved for live pulse dots & confirmation chips
```

### Color Rules
1. **Dominant Tone:** `INK + PAPER + CORAL RED` anchor the visual brand.
2. **One Color Idea Per Section:** Each section possesses a clear chromatic anchor (e.g. Hero on Warm Yellow/Paper, Section 4 on Ink, Section 5 on Pale Lilac/Blue, Section 8 on Ink).
3. **Restraint Over Loudness:** Eliminate rainbow clashing.

## 4. Key Section Architecture

### Hero: The Reel Field
* Editorial left/right composition.
* Left: Display H1 *"Know what works. Make more of it."*, narrative subhead, and Primary CTA.
* Right: **The Reel Field**—3 to 5 overlapping art-directed Reel Specimens rendered in 2.5D perspective.
* Motion: Smooth entrance; V-Lens sweeps the primary Reel; signal markers reveal; settles gracefully.
* Negative space replaces excessive decorative color waves.

### Signature Section: Scan, Don't Explode ("See What's Inside the Reel")
* Pinned 100vh viewport sequence over a 200vh scroll track.
* **0–20%:** One oversized monolithic Reel Specimen occupies center stage.
* **20–40%:** The V-Lens enters from the top edge.
* **40–65%:** V-Lens sweeps vertically through the Reel, illuminating Hook, Captions, Audio Waveform, and Cut Points inside the content.
* **65–80%:** The multi-track Signal Ribbon forms along the right edge of the Reel.
* **80–100%:** 2 companion Reel Specimens slide into perspective; matching Signal Ribbon tracks highlight, revealing the **Shared Pattern**.
* **Zero UI Explosion:** No rectangular card explosion, no generic blueprint admin panel. Content remains content.

### Section 3: The Viralyst Analysis Loop ("How It Works")
* 4 cohesive stages of one unified visual system:
  1. `01 / LEARN`: Brand context fragments converge into the V-Lens.
  2. `02 / FIND`: Multi-Reel field ingested from TikTok, Reels, Shorts.
  3. `03 / READ`: V-Lens scans media and reveals structural Signal Ribbons.
  4. `04 / MAKE`: Content Blueprint forms (Hook, Beats, Visual Direction).

### Section 5: What Viralyst Reads
* Central Reel Specimen anchored in the center.
* 5 custom **Signal Totems**:
  1. `Visual`: Filmstrip / frame-change sequence.
  2. `Words`: Transcript ribbon with emphasized hook phrase.
  3. `Captions`: Kinetic caption blocks aligned in time.
  4. `Audio`: Waveform strand with BPM metric.
  5. `Edit`: Cut/pacing timeline with velocity markers.
* Strict 0px horizontal overflow across all devices.

### Section 8: Viralyst Manager Studio
* Dedicated deep INK (`#0B0B0F`) section answering *"What should we make next?"*.
* Left: Value proposition, Launch CTA, 4 active levers (Hook, Visual, Pacing, Audio).
* Right: High-confidence Synthesized Content Direction card.
